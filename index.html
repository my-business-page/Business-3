Frangipani Wellness Spa keeps Javanese heritage alive through traditional therapy. Its story began in 2006, inspired by knowledge gathered in Indonesia and carried into the heart of Kampong Gelam.

Every ritual is an invitation to slow down — from deep, rhythmic massage to botanical oils and restorative beauty treatments.
