Frangipani's signature identity is rooted in traditional Javanese massage, homemade herbal oils and a belief that healing should be practiced with care.

01 / The method

Pressure
with purpose.

The spa describes its traditional Javanese massage as an ancient deep-tissue technique using all parts of the hand and intense strokes. Long movements, palm pressure and thumb pressure are adapted to the guest.

02 / Adaptation

Strong when
you need it.

Not every body asks for the same rhythm. Frangipani says therapists can adjust intensity and move toward a smoother Balinese-style massage when a more relaxed experience is preferred.

03 / The oils

Handmade
healing oils.

The spa makes its own herbal and healing oils. Its frangipani oil is a closely held recipe, prepared through a lengthy infusion process rather than treated as a generic retail product.

The spa describes preserving a particular oil for around 40 days so ingredients can infuse.
