Kampong Gelam · A living story

Born from
a journey.

Frangipani's story is inseparable from Moe Salem's travels, his connection with Kampong Gelam and his determination to keep Javanese wellness knowledge alive.

01 / 2006

A spa with
roots.

Frangipani Wellness Spa opened in 2006. Founder Moe Salem developed a passion for meditation and healing during travels through places including Medan, Surabaya and Cirebon, where he learned from local elders about Javanese wellness traditions.

02 / Kampong Gelam

Place is
part of it.

The spa has moved between several Kampong Gelam locations over the years and relocated to 73 Aliwal Street in 2022. For Moe, the precinct's Bugis, Javanese, Indian Muslim and Arab history is part of what makes the place special.

03 / Passing it on

Knowledge
worth keeping.

Moe has spoken about ways to pass on knowledge of herbal oils and jamu, reflecting the spa's wider aim of preserving a living tradition rather than treating wellness as a disposable trend.
